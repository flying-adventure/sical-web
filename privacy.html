Privacy

  • The developer collects no information and runs no server. There are no analytics, ads, or trackers.
  • This page is hosted on GitHub Pages. Its operator (GitHub) may keep access logs, such as IP addresses, for security and similar purposes.
  • Your events are stored on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own private iCloud storage. The developer cannot see them.
  • A share link contains the events themselves. Anyone who has the link can read them, and the service you send it through (messenger, mail) handles the link like any other message content. The part after the # is not sent to the web host; it is decoded only in the viewer's browser.
